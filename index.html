Our story

A room built for the punchline

Brick walls, red curtains, a low ceiling and a crowd close enough to see the comic sweat. Founder and host Sima Chikavhu built the club to put comedians and audiences in the same small room, on purpose.

Established names try new material next to the circuit's emerging talent — and on any given night you might catch both.
