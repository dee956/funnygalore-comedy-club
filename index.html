Our story

A room built for the punchline

Sima's Funny Galore is a proper comedy club — not a bar with a mic in the corner. Brick walls, red curtains, a low ceiling and a crowd close enough to see the comic sweat. Founder Sima Chikavhu hosts most nights on the mic.

Since opening in Emmarentia we've put South Africa's biggest names on the same small stage as the circuit's newest — and sent a lot of people home with sore faces.
