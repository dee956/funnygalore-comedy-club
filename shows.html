Upcoming Events

Headline specials, weekly residencies and one-off nights — all at 111 Lotsani Lane. Grab tickets early; the room is small.

Every show at Funny Galore is ticketed in advance — the room is small and most nights sell out. Here's how it works.
  • Tickets are sold through Webtickets. You'll get a confirmation email with your ticket attached — check your spam/junk folder if it doesn't arrive.
  • Bring your ticket (on your phone is fine) and photo ID. The box office can look you up on the door if needed.
  • Doors open an hour before the show. Come early — seating is general admission and the good seats go first.
  • Shows are 18+ unless the event says otherwise. Latecomers are seated at a break at the team's discretion.
