About

A comedy club, on purpose

Sima's Funny Galore is a brick-walled, red-curtained room in Emmarentia built for one thing: people watching stand-up, up close.

The room

Downstairs at 111 Lotsani Lane you'll find low ceilings, exposed brick, heavy red curtains and a tufted panel behind the mic. It holds about 90 people, which means there's nowhere to hide — for the audience or the comic.

That's the point. A theatre is too formal and a bar is too loud. This room sits in between: intimate, intentional, and built so the punchline lands on everyone at once.

In Sima's words

The interview

Sima is a comedian and promoter with years on the Johannesburg circuit, and the club is built around one belief: intimacy and intent. A theatre is too formal, a bar is too loud — this room sits in between.

The hard part, in Sima's telling, is filling a small room 150-plus nights a year while keeping pricing fair for the crowd and the comics. The answer is the weekly rhythm: established headliners trying new material next to emerging acts, every single week.
