About

A comedy club, on purpose

A brick-walled, red-curtained room in Emmarentia built for one thing: people watching stand-up, up close.

The room

Nowhere to hide

111 Lotsani Lane is a low-ceilinged room with exposed brick, heavy red curtains and a tufted panel behind the mic. It holds roughly 90 people — close enough that the audience and the comic are in it together.

In Sima's words
"A theatre is too formal, a bar is too loud. This room sits right in the middle — intimate, intentional, and honest about the fact that comedy is a live sport."Sima Chikavhu, founder & host

Sima is a comedian and promoter with years on the Johannesburg circuit. The challenge she set herself: fill a small room 150-plus nights a year, keep the pricing fair, and put emerging talent next to established headliners every single week.
